title: This, I Promise
date: 2026-08-23
image: images/philosophore-img.png
---
*I don't promise to fix your sadness, but I promise to sit with you in it.*

*I won't try to always be right, but I will always try to understand.*

*I may not have the perfect words, but I'll hold your hand through every silence.*

*I can't promise forever, because forever is made of fragile moments*

*but I will choose you, in every one of them.*

*I promise to love you in quiet ways in small notes, sleepy hugs, soft kisses, and late-night laughs over nothing.*

*I promise to see your soul even on the days you feel invisible.*

*When life gets heavy, I'll carry it with you.*

*When your smile fades, I'll lend you mine.*

*Even if the world grows quiet, my love for you will be loud faithful, gentle, and always returning.*

*This, I promise*
